# JutJut comprehensive UI/UX audit — October 2026

**Audit status:** Source-led consolidation of eight specialist audits. **No source, configuration, data, payment, or live-preview state was changed.** The public preview was observed as a coming-soon/waitlist landing page. Unless marked otherwise, findings are **source-review inferences** rather than authenticated end-to-end reproductions.

> **Evidence language used in this report**
>
> - **Source-review inference** — behavior follows from the reviewed current code, but was not executed against production data.
> - **Not present in current code** — the reviewed UI/router/schema implementation contains no identified feature or route for the stated capability. This is not the same as “not tested.”
> - **Not tested** — an available or potential behavior was deliberately not exercised because credentials, third-party access, safe test data, devices, or financial side effects were unavailable/unsafe.
> - **Live smoke check** — read-only unauthenticated preview/HTTP/render observation only; it does not establish authenticated workflow behavior.

## 1. Executive summary

### UX health score: **3.5 / 10**

JutJut has a clear, mobile-friendly public value proposition and several thoughtful production-oriented components (role-specific waitlist framing, protected procedures, QR expiry states, guided employer onboarding, and practicals lifecycle states). However, the current experience has a substantial **trust-to-transaction gap**: the public product is correctly positioned as coming soon, while many signed-in surfaces still mix durable data with seeded/local demos; conversion, policy, account, privacy, safety, billing, and accessibility paths are materially incomplete or inconsistent. The score reflects the **current code and supplied preview**, not a claim that every development-only capability is publicly available.

### Top five risks

1. **Critical payment integrity and revenue leakage.** Stripe checkout has a payload mismatch; Pin purchase settlement can grant credits twice; no Stripe webhook is registered despite the displayed endpoint; refunds are only local status changes; and concurrent posting can overspend credits (**RECR-002/003/004/006; BILL-02/03/04**).
2. **Account and authorization controls are insufficient for a student-facing service.** OAuth state is not browser-bound and lacks app-side PKCE; suspension is not enforced; sessions last a year without central revocation; and cross-student/cross-school practical and kit access checks are missing (**AUTH-01/05/06; STU-11; INST-F03/F04**).
3. **Trust is undermined by inaccurate public and in-product promises.** Discovery pricing/ABN conflicts, footer policy/contact links go to waitlist, waitlist signup sends an apparently nonfunctional account-verification link, and deletion/direct-university-submission claims lack matching current code (**DISC-001; TRUST-002/008; WAITLIST-007; FEATURE-006**).
4. **Students can be misled by demo-like surfaces presented as operational.** A signed-in student can see a seeded Alex Mercer identity, local-only “verified” Kit actions, a rendered hard-coded feed, fabricated job attributes, and simulated university/accessibility behavior (**ONB-01/STU-01/02/03/04/08; INST-F07**).
5. **Accessibility and operational resilience block inclusive completion.** Focus visibility, keyboard access, disclosure state, form association/errors, modal behavior and accessible names are inconsistent; generic support/error reporting, upload failure handling, and critical browser/provider test coverage are insufficient (**A11Y-01–12; REL-01/02; TEST-01**).

### Top five strengths

1. **Clear pre-signup hierarchy:** the public first viewport makes launch status, student/employer paths, student-free framing, and role-specific waitlist CTAs legible, including at the reviewed 390×844 mobile render.
2. **Useful coming-soon conversion mechanics:** employer CTAs preselect the employer role; landing pricing is concise and matches the protected $15/$50 credit-pack source; waitlist validation, duplicate handling and server-side Zod validation exist.
3. **Meaningful protected-data foundations:** protected tRPC procedures centrally reject unauthenticated callers; admin routes are role-protected; school/practicals have institution/member gates; unauthenticated upload and Drop API smoke checks returned 401.
4. **Several mature workflow building blocks:** employer profile onboarding, durable job listing exclusion of inactive/expired roles, Drop loading/empty/QR-expiry states, and a server-enforced practicals lifecycle are present.
5. **Positive accessibility intent and resilient patterns:** `lang`, viewport, skip links, FAQ expanded state, Radix dialog/button primitives, responsive grids/table scroll wrappers, reduced-confetti behavior, and several explicit retry/empty/error states provide a strong base for remediation.
